APPLY TO REMILIA CORPORATION

Remilia is building the new internet. The new internet is hypertextual, realtime, multiplayer, recomposable, on a structured graph. We are actively recruiting talented engineers, designers and creatives to deliver this mission.

Our organizational philosophy is small, mixed squads on a sprint rhythm, blending product, design and engineering. Success at Remilia requires autonomy, grit, ownership, speed, taste, ambition, and genuine interest in the craft of your field.
